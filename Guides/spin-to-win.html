Spin to Win

An all-around ranged build with strong single-target and multi-target damage, built around balancing the three elemental damage stats and letting Ilonica's Spirit Staff auto-cast bolts for you.

Core Principle

Elemental Damage

There are three elemental damage stats in the game: Lightning , Fire , and Ice — coming mainly from their respective combos. Your Highest Elemental Damage is simply your highest value across the three. The Elemental combo scales your Highest Elemental Damage, adding to whichever stat is currently on top.

Now, what if all three are equal? Here's the trick: if all three are equal, Highest Elemental Damage affects all three — three times the value, for balance. This balance — fragile as it is — is our core principle.

The Spinner

Our weapon of choice is the Staff, upgraded at the first anvil into the Flower Staff. The Flower Staff changes our basic attack into an infinite spinner — drastically reducing damage but giving continuous output (the special attack is unchanged). From there, another anvil takes us to Ilonica's Spirit Staff — our Spinner.

Grimoire Haste

This is one of our most important stats. Grimoire Haste affects the recovery of our grimoires — and our bolts recover on their own. We cast them at our Attack Speed, sure, but if they're on cooldown, we're out of luck. Academy has us covered here, through both its combo effects and its items.

Note — heavily investing in Grimoire Haste alone is a mistake: Attack Speed will soon fall behind the cooldowns of the three bolts, since they recover independently of one another.
Build Details

Core Items

We already have our Ilonica's Spirit Staff and the three bolt grimoires: . What else?

The two must-picks are Bicorne Pipe and Mouse Mage's Pendant. The first lets us shoot 3 bolts instead of 1 (getting it to +5 is a priority since it lowers damage reduction); the second is simple homing — a great QoL that also greatly increases our single-target damage from any range.

Onto the scaling — as mentioned in Pros, we can scale our defenses and our damage climbs with them. Meet Academy Fountain Pen and Shield Bag. The first gets us a % damage boost to our three books through evasion and also helps with cooldowns; the second is a flat increase to our elemental stats — conveniently all three at once, no downsides. Getting them to MAX is equally important, so we're ramping up damage, not squeezing it out.

Academy Brigandine is amazing — it gives so much Grimoire Haste with levels, along with some defense, and that's not all: Academy Brigandine and Shield Bag are both Academy and Guardian , so we get Guardian going for free — extra defense, extra damage.

Honorable mentions: Multipurpose Belt — +1 to all three elemental stats for each artifact in the top inventory row (up to +6 each). It's also Mystic , which is nice and helps with its x2 buff on inventory slots to get artifacts to MAX easier. Second honorable mention is Beast's Heart, a Guardian combo item that gives a lot of defense with levels, along with some Universal Damage Boost and Luck.

Talents

Ingenuity 20 Defense 5 Luck 5

Ingenuity at 20 gives us 20 Max HP and 10 Evasion (remember Academy Fountain Pen), but what we care about here is +7% Universal Damage Boost for each active combo type — and we'll have a lot of combos active with this build.

Luck at 5 gives a nice +2 rerolls to help find our Elementalist Miracle, alongside +1 Wishing Fountain Capacity — this lets us bring one Yellow item into the run. I suggest Bicorne Pipe.

Defense 5 is an all-around Foundation tablet from the start — 5 extra defense (remember Shield Bag) — and it also helps with levels on our items.

Combos

Ingenuity lets us scale our damage with active combos, so let's see how far we can take it.

Academy is our main combo — we force it, nothing to discuss here.

Guardian requires 2 items to activate, and we already have Shield Bag and Academy Brigandine, so it's covered.

Shadow — 2 items to activate; the first is our Academy Fountain Pen, so pick any other and you're good to go.

Magitech , Ember and Glacier all need 2 items, and our bolt grimoires cover the first item on all three. Enter the Jellyfishes — they fill in the missing last item from each elemental combo. Why Jellyfish? Because they're also Companion , so we get that combo for free.

Mystic is easy if you have Multipurpose Belt — pick any extra Mystic item and the combo is live.

Lake though is a scam — it requires 3 items to activate and there's little to no Lake items that fit our build. Skip.

Elemental is amazing since it scales Highest Elemental Damage and needs only 2 items — worth going for, but in my experience too hard to come by.

Any other 2-item combos can be picked up and used sparingly, or you can even try opting into Lake — nothing specific here, it's all about what the game gives you.

How Not to Ruin Your Damage

Remember, we must keep Lightning , Fire , and Ice stats in sync at all times. This means on their respective combos (Ember, Glacier and Magitech) we can't go above 3 items, since 4 gives a flat boost to that specific stat. Also beware items that give temporary buffs to a certain element — these will ruin everything too. Potions which give flat elemental stats are completely out of the question: easy to mess up, almost impossible to recover.

Shrines

Shrines let you pick either Max HP or Defense — opting into Defense is highly recommended, since Shield Bag and Ingenuity already give us Max HP.

Costumes

For our "Class" we have two strong options: Braided Hair Rabbit or Brown Rabbit. The first gives Attack Speed, which boosts our spinner and damage; the second is Evasion-focused. I'd recommend Brown Rabbit, since we can scale damage through evasion with Academy Fountain Pen, and it also makes starting runs more comfortable since the build does require some rare items.
